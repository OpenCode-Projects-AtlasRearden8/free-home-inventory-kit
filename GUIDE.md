# Welcome! (This is the friendly part.)

Hi — you picked up the Free Home Inventory Kit. Good instinct. Here's the
whole idea in one breath:

> You're going to print a few sheets, walk around your house with a pen for
> a little while, and end up with a paper binder that would save your family
> *weeks* of stress after a fire, burglary, or pipe leak.

That's it. No app. No account. No "sync" that quietly gives your photos to a
corner of the internet you'll never think about again.

## The one promise that matters

**Nothing here leaves your home.** These are printable paper pages. Fill
them by hand. Store them in a drawer or a binder. If you want to type into
them, you can fill the HTML forms in your browser and print — and your
browser never sends anything anywhere, because we wrote this with plain
HTML/CSS: no JavaScript, no web fonts, no links to the outside. It works
with the Wi-Fi router unplugged.

## The kit, piece by piece

| Sheet | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Room-by-Room Inventory Log** | The heart of it. One sheet per room. Big stuff first, small stuff as patience allows. |
| **Photo Caption Cards** | Print 4 cards to a page, tape on a photo, write the story. Worth hiring a photographer? No. Worth 12 phone photos? Yes. |
| **Serial Number Tracker** | For anything with a label: laptops, TVs, appliances, bikes. Insurance people *love* serial numbers. |
| **Policy & Contacts One-Pager** | The "who do I call at 2 a.m." page, including where your important papers live. |
| **"Check the Binder" Reminder Card** | One honest hour a year keeps all of this true. This page is the nudge. |

## How to get started tonight (the 20-minute version)

1. Print **one** copy of each sheet. (Below: how to print, even without Chrome.)
2. Do the kitchen and the living room on the log sheet. Guess prices. Guessing is allowed.
3. Snap a phone photo of one wall per room. That's arguably the single most
   valuable thing anyone in this project tells you.
4. Put the sheets in a folder marked "Home." You've done more than most people ever will.

Everything else — every room, serial numbers, the contacts page — can trickle
in whenever you feel like it. This kit is designed to tolerate being left
half-finished and still be useful.

## How to print the sheets

**Easiest:** open any `sheets/*.html` file in your browser (double-click it),
then press **Ctrl+P** (Cmd+P on Mac) and choose "Save as PDF" or your printer.
Everything is already laid out for US Letter paper; A4 prints fine too.

**Fully automatic:** from a terminal, run:

```
python3 build.py
```

That prints every sheet to a PDF in `output/`. It uses the Chrome or Chromium
browser already on your computer, headlessly — it does not download or
install anything. Zero signups, zero accounts, zero network calls.

## A few honest words about insurance and estates

We keep it plain: this kit helps you *document* what you own. It is not
legal or insurance advice, and a paper binder doesn't replace reading your
actual policy. But when something goes wrong, the person standing in the
kitchen with a numbered list and photos is the person whose claim gets paid
faster. That's the whole magic trick.
