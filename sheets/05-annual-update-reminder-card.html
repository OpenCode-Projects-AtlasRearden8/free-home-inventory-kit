Your once-a-year hour

  • Walk room by room with the log sheet. Add anything new. Cross out anything gone.
  • Snap fresh phone photos of the big-ticket items and any new purchases.
  • Re-check serial numbers that changed (upgraded laptop? new bike? new TV?).
  • Peel off any receipts that don't matter anymore — recycle without guilt.
  • Double-check phone numbers on the Policy & Contacts page. Agents retire; companies merge.
  • Confirm a trusted person still knows where the binder lives.

Cut along the box, fold in half, and it’s a wallet- or calendar-sized card. Or don't cut — sticking the whole page on the fridge works beautifully too.
